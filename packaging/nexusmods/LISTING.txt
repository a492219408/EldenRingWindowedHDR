Mod Name
========
Elden Ring Native Windowed HDR

Short description (TEXT, 266 characters)
========================================
Enables Elden Ring's native HDR rendering in Borderless Windowed and Windowed modes through ModEngine3, including in-game toggling and saved-state restoration. Requires an HDR-capable display with Windows HDR enabled and the supported game version. Offline use only.
